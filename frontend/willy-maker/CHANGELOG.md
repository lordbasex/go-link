# Willy Maker changelog

Willy Maker, go-link's arcade game maker (maker.go-link.org). Newest first. Its entries before the move to its own site were in go-link's [CHANGELOG.md](../../CHANGELOG.md), under the website's sections, and were moved here with their versions (the go-link release that published them).

## [Unreleased]

### Added

- **go-link HD (beta) in Export:** a platformer downloads as a go-link HD package (`.glhd`): manifest, its first level as one letter per cell (blocks, one-way platforms, coins, checkpoints, the goal, enemies, the start) and the city's tiles (`io/glhdExport.ts`, same bytes for the same project); a go-link with go-link HD's core lists it and plays it in a game room. Texts in English, Spanish and Portuguese; tests.

### Docs

- **The AI playtester's specification** ([docs/willy-maker/ai-playtester.md](../../docs/willy-maker/ai-playtester.md)): what Test with AI will do, each item marked Base, Later or Idea (players, training, the report, the editor, genres, data and privacy, QA bots in rooms), with budgets, phases and the decisions taken: a small network trained on each game with the route bot as its teacher, reading the game's state, in the browser, the platformer first. Nothing is built yet.

### Changed

- **Backgrounds closer to the original picture** ([engine.md](../../docs/willy-maker/engine.md#layer-palettes)): when a background's colors do not fit the board's 32 palettes of 15 exactly, the palettes are refined after the first pass (each tile to the palette that shows it best, each palette's colors chosen again from its tiles). On a level of six image AI pictures the patches of the wrong tone are gone and the pixels visibly off went from 54 % to 42 %, with lamps and neon kept sharp. Insert background and Add scene use it; a background already in a game improves when its pictures are inserted again.
- **Your characters instead of Willy**: in Characters, hovering an animation that already has frames plays your character's own frames (Willy stays the example for the ones without), and the editor draws your own hero, enemies, civilians and pickups with their sprite on the canvas, in the sprite picker and in Properties, where it showed boxes. Each animation's dot is green once it has frames and grey while it has none (it was always grey).

### Added

- **Edit the background's scenes on the canvas** ([editor.md](../../docs/willy-maker/editor.md#canvas)): with the background selected, each scene has a box: drag it to move the scene, over or under its neighbour, and drag the picked scene's side edges to cut them off, so the seam between two pictures hides under the next one. Properties adds Cut left, Cut right and Replace picture for each scene, and Behind / In front now change only which scene is drawn over the other. The level now ends where its scenes end, so overlapping them no longer leaves an empty strip at the end (a zone or an object is never cut off).
- **Foreground** ([editor.md](../../docs/willy-maker/editor.md#canvas), [engine.md](../../docs/willy-maker/engine.md#the-engine), `wm_data` 29): Insert › Foreground puts a picture in front of the players that moves faster than the camera (110-250 %), like a lamp post passing close to the screen; up to 16 pieces in one palette of 15 colors, drawn first in the board's sprite table. The canvas shows each piece where it is when the camera's middle reaches it; drag it, set its place, height and speed in Properties, and find it first in Layers. Play mode and the ROM draw it the same way, and the lab run `front-walk.json` matches the real core at tolerance 0.
- **Depth bands in the editor** ([editor.md](../../docs/willy-maker/editor.md#canvas)): the background's Properties has a Depth section to add up to 4 bands of rows that move slower than the camera in the game (the parallax bands both engines already drew, until now only set in the classic editor), and the canvas marks them with their speed while the background is selected.
- **Background scenes you can adjust and line up** ([editor.md](../../docs/willy-maker/editor.md#canvas)): each picture of the background (Insert background, Add scene) is kept as a scene, and the background is laid out from them again whenever one changes. With the background selected, Properties lists the scenes with their X, Down and Size %, their order and Remove, and **Line up the floor** moves each one so its wall line meets the one before it where they join (for pictures an image AI drew at slightly different heights).
- **Far background with parallax** ([editor.md](../../docs/willy-maker/editor.md#canvas)): Insert › Far background puts a skyline on the board's far layer, repeated across the level, and the game moves it at half speed behind the background; a background picture with an image AI's magenta sky lets it show through (the magenta becomes see-through). Layers lists it with a remove button. Add scene now goes right after the end of the background's art, so a level wider than its art is filled from where the art ends.
- **Add scene and the timeline** ([editor.md](../../docs/willy-maker/editor.md#canvas)): after the level's right end, **+ Add scene** puts another picture (chosen or dropped there) after the background and the level grows by its width, so the scenery goes on; **Repeat** adds the same art again with no new tiles; one undo step each. Under the canvas, the **timeline** is back (the classic editor's minimap): the whole level small, its screens numbered, its sections, and a box on what the view shows; click or drag it to move the view.
- **The board's spec sheet in New game** ([hardware.md](../../docs/rom/hardware.md#which-cps-1)): choosing the CPS-1 shows which of its boards a game is built for (the 1992 QSound board, as the `slammast` set), its 68000 at 10 MHz as the emulator runs it (12 MHz on the real board, and why that is fine), the Z80 and QSound, the screen, the colors, the sprites, the layers, the palettes, the memory and the controls of the buttons picked; the board's card says 68000 · 10 MHz · QSound. The data lives in the board profile (`board/cps1.ts` `hardware`).
- **A pixel editor for characters' animations** ([editor.md](../../docs/willy-maker/editor.md#pixel-editor)), laid out like Scratch's costume editor: the animation's frames on the left (new, duplicate, move, remove), pencil, eraser, bucket, pick a color, line, rectangle and ellipse, undo and redo, flips, zoom and a pixel grid, the 16 px zones and the feet point, onion skin, layers per frame (show, hide, lock, rename, reorder, merge, and a shirt layer whose colors are recolored for players 2 to 4), vector layers like Scratch's (rectangle, ellipse, line and polygon with fill and outline, select, move, resize, recolor, forward and backward, and Convert to bitmap; the frame and layer buttons fit their columns), more tools (mirror drawing, select pixels to move, flip, turn, cut, copy and paste, replace a color in a layer or the whole animation, an automatic outline, each zone's count over the whole animation and Bring down to 15 colors, a canvas size that keeps the drawing on its bottom row, a picture opened or pasted into a new layer fitted to the frame, an SVG as a crisp picture or as shapes you can edit, the board's colors, palette size and zone height taken from the game's board profile, and the animation previewed in the first level), a frame's picture under an animation is the button that opens it (with a pencil on it) and the button below says Edit frames when the animation has frames, colors snapped to the board's, the character's colors, each zone's count of its 15, and the animation playing. It opens from a frame's ✎, **Draw frames** under an animation or **Draw from scratch** for a character with no sheet; the frames drawn on or drawn new are saved with the character and used everywhere (animations, play mode, the ROM), so retouching what an image AI drew, or drawing a character from nothing, needs no other program.
- **A game's own sound** ([engine.md](../../docs/willy-maker/engine.md#a-games-own-sound)): the Game tab's new **Sound** card gives each effect (shot, enemy down, rescue…) a recipe of its own from a library, the music of each screen (title, level, level clear, continue, game over), and the game's own songs in the tracker form the QSound chip plays, each heard right there. Four tango instruments (bandoneon, violin, pizzicato, piano) join the arcade ones. A tune in **ABC notation** becomes a song in one click, arranged as a tango (bass on the 3-3-2, piano stabs, a violin pad, the bass's drag and a "chan-chan" ending). The **tango pack** puts it all in at once: La Cumparsita (1916, public domain, the tune only) on the title, a milonga on the level and effects to match. Play mode and the ROM play the same; the review stops Create ROM on a song that cannot play or music that does not fit the chip's 16 KB.
- **Own characters in the new editor's level**: the sprite picker offers the game's own enemy and civilian characters first, a pickup gets a **Picture** (one of the game's characters) in Properties, and **Image AI prompts…** in the tools panel opens the prompt dialog for pictures made with an image AI. Play mode draws the own enemies from their pictures, as the ROM does.
- **An end-to-end test with image AI art** (`e2e/tests/willy-maker-games.spec.ts`, [validation.md](../../docs/willy-maker/validation.md)): two games, a platform shooter and a beat 'em up, made in the new editor from pictures ChatGPT drew from Willy Maker's own prompts (`e2e/fixtures/maker-art/PROMPTS.md`), from the wizard to a ROM that boots on the board model, with a screenshot and a report of every step.
- **The new editor is the default** ([editor.md](../../docs/willy-maker/editor.md#my-games)): maker.go-link.org/ is My games in its look (games as cards with their first screen; open, duplicate, download .zip, delete; New game opens the new wizard; Open .zip; Watch demo, which saves nothing and goes back home on Exit), and maker.go-link.org/<game id> opens the game in it. `?editor=classic` keeps a browser on the classic editor and home.
- **The new editor's Characters, Game, Menus and Export** (`?editor=next`, [editor.md](../../docs/willy-maker/editor.md#workspaces)): the four workspaces take the new look in light and dark (square buttons, inputs and choices, the Archivo font, the red accent), where they showed the classic editor's dark capsules over the light page. The texts of every part are checked in English, Spanish and Portuguese.
- **The new editor's New game wizard and demo** (`?editor=next`, [editor.md](../../docs/willy-maker/editor.md#new-game-wizard-and-demo)): six steps (type, board and buttons, name and players, background, hero, summary) make the game with its genre's rules and a first level over the example picture, your own picture or a plain floor. Watch demo makes a level in front of you on a game that is never saved, with the real menus and picker, and plays it to the end; then Create my game, Watch again or Keep editing it.
- **Play inside the new editor** (`?editor=next`, [editor.md](../../docs/willy-maker/editor.md#playing)): Play runs the level in the game's real play mode, the same rules as the ROM, as the board's screen with its sprites, art, HUD and sound, under a bar with the keys, lives, score, enemies and people rescued, and "Level complete!" at the end. The debug chips drive the screen frame, collision, hitboxes, camera, FPS and slow motion. Esc or Stop goes back to building.
- **The new editor's Properties and shortcuts** (`?editor=next`, [editor.md](../../docs/willy-maker/editor.md#properties)): a zone's name, kind, place, size and a breakable zone's hits; a character's name, sprite, place and the side it faces; the background's picture, Replace and Remove; what is typed in one field is one undo step. Every shortcut of the design works (undo and redo, duplicate, group, new group, rename, delete, the arrows to move by one or four cells, zoom, panels, insert background, play) and `?` lists them all, from the same table the keys use. Changing a character's sprite keeps the name you gave it.
- **The new editor's Layers panel** (`?editor=next`, [editor.md](../../docs/willy-maker/editor.md#layers-and-groups)): the level's groups with their zones and characters, and the background. Show, hide and lock layers and whole groups (hidden ones leave the game too: hidden zones are not in the collision layer, and hidden characters, enemies and items are left out of play mode and the ROM), rename in place with a double click, drag layers between groups, make a group active for what comes next, and a menu per group (new, move up or down, ungroup, delete with its content). The canvas menu's Rename, Group in a new group and Move to group work now.
- **The new editor's canvas** (`?editor=next`, [editor.md](../../docs/willy-maker/editor.md#canvas)): the level's art, its zones by kind and its characters, enemies and items over it, the screen frame and the grid. Select and move (and resize zones by their corner), Draw zone (a rectangle of the chosen kind on the 16 px grid), Erase, and the Hand or Space to pan; ⌘ + wheel zooms around the pointer, ⌘0 fits, ⌘1 is 100 %. A right click opens the context menu (insert a background, character, enemy or object there; draw a zone; change a zone's kind; turn, duplicate or delete; lock or remove the background). The sprite picker places heroes, enemies and objects or changes an object's sprite. Insert background (or a picture dropped on the canvas, or ⌘O) fits a picture of your own to the board as the level's play layer, so the zones you draw on it stay on it in the game. An empty level offers an example level: a neon city with its floors, platforms, ladder, crate and hazard already marked. The collision layer is drawn from the zones on every change, so play mode and the ROM read what the canvas shows; zones are made again from the collision layer when the classic editor painted it.
- **Zones and layer groups in the project file**: a level can hold zones (rectangles that say what a part of the level is: floor, platform, ladder, crate, breakable, hazard, water) and the layers panel's groups. Its collision layer is drawn from the visible zones, so play mode and the ROM read the same cells; a level made before gets its zones from its collision cells (the Buenos Aires template's zones draw back exactly its cells). Read and checked on load; the new editor will draw them.
- **A new editor is being built**, next to the classic one, which stays the default: open a game with `?editor=next` to try it (`?editor=classic` goes back; [docs/willy-maker/editor.md](../../docs/willy-maker/editor.md)). Its first step is the layout: a full-screen workspace in the Modernist look (square corners, the Archivo font served by the site, light and dark, Lucide icons) with the header (workspaces, language, panels, theme, undo, redo, Play), the tools panel with the first steps (or its icon rail with flyout menus), the options bar (zone kinds, debug overlays, View, grid size, zoom), the Properties and Layers panel and the footer (cursor place, saved state). Panels resize by their edge and hide with Tab, ⇧Tab, F6 and F7; tools and zone kinds have their keys (V, B, E, H, 1-6). The canvas's content, layers, properties and play come in the next steps.

### Fixed

- **Every frame of your characters' animations, in play mode and in the ROM** ([engine.md](../../docs/willy-maker/engine.md#the-players-looks), `wm_data` version 28): walking used the run's animation at half speed, so a character with only a walk (nine frames at 12 fps) walked at 6 fps and seemed to slide, and some moves cut their animations short (Willy's turn showed 2 of its 6 frames). Now walking plays the walk at its own speed and running the run (or the walk twice as fast), a timed move (knife, land, turn, jump kick, thumbs up) shows all its own frames within the move, and the jump spreads its frames over rising and falling, whatever number of frames each animation has. Play mode and the ROM share the rules (`engine/anims.ts`); a nine frame walk matches the real core at tolerance 0 (`rom/tools/lab/runs/hero-walk.json`).
- **The ROM showed the own enemies, civilians and pickups in a recruit's shirt colors**: the engine loaded only the players' looks' palettes; it loads every own look's now (checked on the real core at tolerance 0, and the lab's runs still start on frame 163).
- The Menus tab's music note no longer says play mode is silent (it plays the music since T-26).
- **Create ROM drew the own enemies, civilians and pickup pictures as the engine's**: it loaded only the pictures of the players' heroes; it loads every character the game draws now.
- **Play in the new editor started the game again every few frames** (a new list of the heroes' heights on every redraw), so player 1 could not get far and blinked as if just joined.
- **Space inside a dialog of the new editor** (the image AI prompts) panned the canvas instead of ticking the box.
- **A sheet from an image AI got its feet point one row below the frame** after shrinking to the board, and the review warned about every character.
- **A picture of one's own left a strip of the level without art** when its width did not end on the 32 px grid the level grows on: it is stretched a few pixels to end there.
- The Create ROM note for a character without a picture no longer says an enemy or item "is drawn as Willy".
- **Play mode's characters on maker.go-link.org**: since Willy Maker moved to its own site, play mode (and the animation previews) drew Willy, the robots and the people as plain boxes, because their pictures stayed on go-link.org. The maker's site serves them again, from the same files.

## [0.1.9] - 2026-10-04

### Added

- **Willy Maker › Play on my go-link opens a room**: one click sends the game to the linked go-link, which opens its room with the real core, and the site takes you there to play.
