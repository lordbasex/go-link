Open source · WebRTC

One link, people connected directly.

go-link is a family of apps that join two or more people browser to browser: games, remote desktop, calls, files and terminals. They meet on one signaling server; after that, everything travels straight between them.

The projects

One ecosystem, five ways to connect

Each project is its own app and its own site, with the same idea underneath: your computer and your friends' browsers talk directly.

Available

Console

Your computer becomes the arcade: it runs the games and streams them live. Friends join with a link and a PIN, from any browser or the phone app.

  • Up to four players, an arcade queue and spectators
  • Voice chat between players, gamepads and touch controls
  • MAME arcade games and go-link HD games
Open Console GitHub
Available · v1.1

Desk

Remote desktop: see another computer's screen and use it with your mouse and keyboard from the browser, to help someone or to reach your own machine. Always with its owner's permission.

  • A 9-digit code, and the owner approves every session
  • Picture and sound live, mouse, keyboard, clipboard and files
  • Nothing to install on the side that connects

TangoOS tangoos.go-link.org

An open-source NAS with a desktop in the browser: disks, RAID, shared folders, files and apps. With TangoConnect you reach it from anywhere, without opening ports.

  • RAID 1, 5, 6 and 10 on Btrfs, with snapshots
  • Windows and Mac sharing, Time Machine and Docker apps
  • Remote access and share links, straight to your NAS

How it works

Meet once, then talk directly

  1. Open the app

    The host runs the project's app on their computer, or opens its page.

  2. Share a link

    Friends get a link, a code or a QR. The signaling server only introduces them, like a doorman.

    915 355 636

  3. Connect directly

    WebRTC links the browsers: picture, sound, input and files travel encrypted, straight between them.
